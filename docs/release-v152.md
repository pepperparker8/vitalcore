# VitalCore v152: Body's green line fitted to real mornings

## Before you use it
- Nothing to run in Supabase or the backend.
- Push from GitHub Desktop, then open the app online on your phone. Settings should show Version 152.

## Changed
- Body is green from 57, not 67. Yellow is 34 to 56, red under 34 as before.
- Why: the cuts assumed a usual morning has HRV and resting heart rate on your usual and a full night's need of sleep, which scores 67.5. Eight weeks of the owner's own mornings (a backup, 1 Sep to 27 Oct 2026) scored a median of 57 on the mornings with sleep, 54 over all scored days, so only 9 of 46 scored days read green.- Green now starts at that median, so a usual morning sits on the green line. Red stays at 34, which is about one scored day in ten.
- The cuts move everything that reads them: the ring colour, the Recovery chart zones, the sheet's words, the 7-day outline (hold below green, recover below 34) and the briefing's bands.
- How Body is worked out does not change: HRV 45 %, resting heart rate 20 %, sleep 35 %, each against your own band.

## Not yet checked
- Only 11 of the 46 scored days had HRV, so most of the eight weeks scored from resting heart rate and sleep. Fit the cut again once a backup holds 8 weeks with HRV on most days.
