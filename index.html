Nervous System Recovery

Log morning resting HR in Measurements to track recovery.
ⓘ Why this matters ▾
Resting HR trend is a proxy for recovery. A rising trend over several days signals incomplete recovery or illness onset, often before you consciously feel it.
Training Fatigue
Acute Training Load — 7-day
—
Connect Intervals.icu to see fatigue.
ⓘ Why this matters ▾
ATL reflects recent training stress. High values are expected during hard blocks. Sustained high ATL without recovery leads to declining performance.
How Fresh You Are
Training Stress Balance (CTL − ATL)
—
Connect Intervals.icu to see freshness.
ⓘ Why this matters ▾
TSB = CTL − ATL. Positive = fresh. Negative = fatigued. Optimal race-day TSB is +5 to +20. Deeply negative signals overtraining risk.
