Why this matters

Fitness (CTL) is your slow-building training base. Fatigue (ATL) is what you have done lately. Form (TSB) is fitness minus fatigue: above zero you are fresh, well below zero you are carrying a lot of tiredness.

ATL reflects recent training stress. High values are expected during hard blocks. Sustained high ATL without recovery leads to declining performance.

TSB = CTL − ATL. Positive = fresh. Negative = fatigued. Optimal race-day TSB is +5 to +20. Deeply negative signals overtraining risk.
